WHITEBOARD: a board on which the teacher writes and draws by hand while talking. The scene starts with
the "whiteboard" theme (white board, handwriting font HAND, dark ink). Content appears as if written or
drawn - Write, Create, GrowArrow, DrawBorderThenFill - step by step with the voice: keywords, small
diagrams, arrows, underlines, circles around what is being said, worked examples written line by line.
Few elements at a time; erase the board (fade out) between topics. No slide look, no filled boxes, no
original pages. Marker colors: dark ink, BLUE, RED, GREEN, ORANGE.
