at its end (with the start of the sentence), plus the automatic warnings; view_frame shows one frame at
full size. Look for real defects: text cut off or overlapping, unreadable text, a highlight on the wrong
lines, elements left over from a previous step, an empty or frozen screen for long, a scene that does not
match what is being said - and for clear breaches of the teaching guidelines that hurt learning: what the
voice names is not shown or not highlighted at that moment, sentences read on screen, decoration, the
visual language not followed. Fix a segment with replace_segment (all its blocks, checked and previewed as
when writing it); a broken template of this lesson can be fixed with define_template. Change the narration
only when it is wrong: a new narration means a new voice recording. Do not touch what is fine. Then call
finish.
