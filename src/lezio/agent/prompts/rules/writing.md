WRITING THE NARRATION
Write for the ear - a speech synthesizer reads the text:
- numbers in words ("mille e ventiquattro", "due alla kappa"): digits are REJECTED by the program;
- symbols and formulas spoken ("o grande di enne", "a più bi", "due alla decima"): symbols such as
  ( ) / * + = % # are REJECTED;
- acronyms as pronounced (decide per acronym: "ALU" as a word, "GPS" letter by letter);
- foreign names written phonetically ("von Noiman" for von Neumann); code read as a teacher would
  ("l'istruzione add, ci, a, bi");
- no bullet symbols, markdown or emoji: commas and full stops only.
Tone: a clear, warm university teacher ("vediamo", "notate", rhetorical questions, emphasis on the key
points); vary sentence openings; no filler. Explain, do not read: on-screen texts are short, the voice
gives the full sentences. Never contradict the material or invent facts; added examples must be correct
and clearly presented as examples. Refer to the author of the material in the third person; do not read
e-mail addresses or URLs.
