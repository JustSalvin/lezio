NARRATION FORMAT
The narration of a segment is a list of pieces. Each piece is one audio file:
  "Testo del pezzo."                  a piece
  {"say": "Testo.", "pause": 2}       a piece followed by 2 seconds of silence (at most 10)
One piece = one idea = one step of the scene: 1-3 sentences, about 8-60 words (more than 70 is rejected).
Short pieces let the scene change with the voice.
